LicoUp Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

简体中文

1. Scope and controller

This policy applies to the official LicoUp application for macOS. LicoUp is an open-source, local-first client. The LicoUp project does not operate an advertising, analytics, or user-profile service for the app. If you connect LicoUp to a third-party agent, model provider, relay, repository, or other service, that service processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

2. Data processed on your Mac

LicoUp may process and store app preferences, local agent configuration and status, conversation content you choose to open or create, local diagnostic records, paired-device records, and metadata about skills already present on your device. This information remains under your control on your Mac unless you explicitly direct LicoUp to send it to a selected destination.

3. User-directed network activity

LicoUp may access the network only to perform a feature you select or configure, such as:

  • communicating with a local or remote agent or model service you selected;
  • sending encrypted content to an approved paired device through a configured relay;
  • checking official release metadata and downloading an update you approve; or
  • opening a link or repository location at your request.

A configured relay receives opaque encrypted payloads and the limited routing fields needed to deliver them; it is not treated as an identity authority. Network destinations can still observe standard connection metadata such as IP address, time, and traffic size. LicoUp does not control a third party's retention or use of data you direct to that party.

4. Data LicoUp does not collect

The LicoUp project does not collect app analytics, advertising identifiers, crash telemetry, precise location, contacts, photos, camera content, or microphone content from the macOS app. The macOS app does not request Camera, Microphone, or Screen Recording permission. LicoUp does not sell personal information, serve advertising, track you across other companies' apps or websites, or contact tracking domains.

5. Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you delete it through LicoUp, remove the corresponding local file or skill, or uninstall the app and its local data. Copies sent to a service at your direction are subject to that service's retention and deletion controls. Removing LicoUp cannot delete copies held by a third party.

6. Security and updates

LicoUp keeps protected endpoint material in platform-controlled local custody where supported. Approved peer transfers are encrypted before relay transport. Official macOS updates are accepted only after integrity, Developer ID signature, hardened-runtime, notarization-ticket, and Gatekeeper checks succeed. No security measure can eliminate every risk; protect your Mac, review destinations before approving a transfer, and install updates from official sources.

7. Children and legal rights

LicoUp is a general-purpose developer tool and is not directed to children. Depending on your location, privacy law may give you rights regarding data held by a service provider. Because the LicoUp project does not receive the app's local data, those rights are normally exercised on your device or with the third party you selected.

8. Changes and contact

A material policy change will be published with the corresponding LicoUp source or release. Questions and privacy reports can be submitted through the LicoUp issue tracker. Do not include secrets, credentials, private conversation content, or other sensitive data in a public issue.

LicoUp 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 11 日

1. 适用范围与数据控制方

本政策适用于官方 LicoUp macOS 应用。LicoUp 是开源、本地优先的客户端；LicoUp 项目不为该应用运营广告、分析或用户画像服务。如果你把 LicoUp 连接到第三方智能体、 模型提供商、中继、代码仓库或其他服务，该服务将依据其自己的条款与隐私政策处理数据。

2. 在 Mac 本地处理的数据

LicoUp 可能在本机处理和保存应用偏好、本地智能体配置与状态、你选择打开或创建的对话内容、 本地诊断记录、已配对设备记录，以及设备上已有技能的元数据。除非你明确要求 LicoUp 将其发送到 选定目的地，否则这些信息持续由你在 Mac 上控制。

3. 由用户发起的网络活动

LicoUp 仅会为你选择或配置的功能访问网络，例如：

  • 与所选本地或远程智能体、模型服务通信；
  • 通过已配置的中继向获准的已配对设备发送加密内容；
  • 检查官方版本元数据，并下载经你同意的更新；
  • 按你的请求打开链接或代码仓库地址。

已配置的中继只接收不透明密文和完成投递所需的有限路由字段，不被视为身份权威。网络目的地仍可能 观察到 IP 地址、时间和流量大小等标准连接元数据。对于你主动发送给第三方的数据，LicoUp 无法控制 该第三方的留存和使用方式。

4. LicoUp 不收集的数据

LicoUp 项目不从 macOS 应用收集使用分析、广告标识符、崩溃遥测、精确位置、通讯录、照片、摄像头 内容或麦克风内容。macOS 应用不申请摄像头、麦克风或屏幕录制权限。LicoUp 不出售个人信息、不投放 广告、不跨其他公司的应用或网站跟踪你，也不访问跟踪域名。

5. 留存与删除

本地信息会保留在设备上，直至你在 LicoUp 中删除、移除相应本地文件或技能，或卸载应用并清除其本地 数据。你主动发送给某项服务的副本受该服务的留存和删除机制约束；卸载 LicoUp 无法删除第三方持有的副本。

6. 安全与更新

在平台支持时，LicoUp 将受保护的端点材料保存在平台控制的本地安全域中；获准的对等传输会在进入中继 前加密。官方 macOS 更新只有在完整性、Developer ID 签名、Hardened Runtime、公证票据和 Gatekeeper 检查全部通过后才会被接受。任何安全措施都无法消除全部风险；请保护你的 Mac、在批准传输前核对目的地， 并只从官方来源安装更新。

7. 儿童与法定权利

LicoUp 是通用开发者工具，并非面向儿童。你所在地区的隐私法律可能赋予你针对服务提供商所持数据的权利。 由于 LicoUp 项目不会接收应用本地数据，这些权利通常应在你的设备上或向你选定的第三方行使。

8. 变更与联系

重大政策变更会随相应 LicoUp 源码或版本发布。隐私问题和报告可提交到 LicoUp 问题跟踪器。 请勿在公开问题中提交密钥、凭据、私人对话或其他敏感数据。
