# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Lico Arc welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, tools, languages,
experience levels, and ways of working. Participate with respect, patience, and
care.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and code without attacking people;
- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting privacy, consent, and security boundaries;
- making space for questions and different viewpoints;
- correcting mistakes openly and without retaliation.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, publication of private data, and sustained disruption are
not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, issues, project
events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not include
unnecessary personal information or publish a report as a public issue.
Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate
action. They should protect reporter privacy, avoid conflicts of interest, and
explain outcomes to the affected people when it is safe to do so.
